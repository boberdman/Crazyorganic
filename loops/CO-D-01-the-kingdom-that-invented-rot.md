## Stage 1 — Topic Brief (Research)

**Pillar:** 1 — The unexplored kingdom (wonder)
**Working title:** The Kingdom That Invented Rot *(kept as written in the backlog. "Invented rot" is a wonder-framing the piece substantiates — fungi are the principal decomposers and the sole complete lignin-degraders — not a contested thesis asserted in the brand's voice. The one place the title could over-reach — the deep-time "before fungi, nothing rotted" origin story — is deliberately quarantined under a Contested stamp and the honesty box, so the present-tense established claim carries the title and the deep-time claim is labeled as disputed.)*
**The big question / hook:** Something has to take the world apart. A dead tree is a tower of locked-up carbon built to resist being eaten — so who breaks it down, and what happens to a planet if nothing can? The answer is a kingdom of life we usually find slightly disgusting, doing the most creative-by-way-of-destructive job on Earth.

**Key points:**
1. Wood is cellulose (orderly sugar chains) + **lignin** (an irregular, 3-D, non-repeating polymer that stiffens wood and shields the cellulose). Lignin's disorder is *why* wood resists decay — no standard bond to attack. [established]
2. **White-rot fungi** (Agaricomycetes) are the principal organisms that fully break lignin down — Floudas 2012's flat line: "the only organisms capable of substantial lignin decay are white rot fungi." Carried with the honest nuance that bacteria/other fungi partially modify lignin (so *not* literal exclusivity). They digest externally (callback to CO-A-03), secreting lignin peroxidase / manganese peroxidase / laccase, which don't cut lignin neatly but set off a free-radical "controlled chemical burn." White rot eats everything incl. lignin (pale stringy residue); brown rot takes the sugars and leaves lignin (brown crumbly cubes). [established]
3. Rot is the **return half of the carbon cycle**: decomposition releases the carbon locked in dead wood/litter back to air and soil and frees the nutrients for new growth. Without it, carbon and nutrients stall — even the coal-gap skeptics concede "absence of lignin decay would have profoundly disrupted the carbon cycle." [established]
4. The honesty/wonder pivot — the **Carboniferous coal-gap ("evolutionary lag") hypothesis**: the idea that ~300 Mya trees had evolved lignin but lignin-eating fungi hadn't caught up, so dead wood piled up unrotted and became coal; the 2012 molecular clock placed the origin of white-rot lignin decay near the end-Carboniferous drop in carbon burial. [**contested**]

**Honesty beat (the "Where we get honest" box):** Nelsen et al. 2016 (PNAS) rejected the coal-gap story on three grounds — (1) fossil wood shows decay was already happening and lignin-degradation isn't unique to white rot; (2) much Carboniferous coal came from lignin-poor lycopsid "bark," so lignin abundance didn't track coal; (3) mass balance — 100 My of undecayed lignin would have catastrophically broken the carbon cycle, not merely made coal. Their alternative: a one-time coincidence of everwet tropical climate + Pangea-assembly tectonics (subsiding foreland basins burying peat). Verdict held openly as **Contested / we don't know yet**; present-tense decomposer role stays **Established**.

**Get-it-right traps handled:**
- **"Only fungi rot wood" is too strong.** Established claim scoped to white-rot fungi as the principal *complete* lignin-degraders; the parenthetical + honesty box carry Nelsen's point that other fungi and bacteria partially degrade/modify lignin.
- **Established vs. Contested firewall on the title.** The fun "invented rot / made the coal" deep-time origin is labeled Contested; the title rests on the present-tense established fact.

**Claims-risk flag:** none for §4 hard lines — no psilocybin/cultivation/sourcing, no health/medical/therapeutic, no legal/financial. Pure Pillar-1 established biology + one Contested paleobiology beat.
**Target format(s):** newsletter + 3 X posts.
